# Poznan

[Official info page](https://visitpoznan.pl/en)

Download [Tour guide PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wxX1Chl0N51Raq9Lu-lhngL-Okb4_FOB/view?usp=drive_link)
- [1] Old market square, must have (4h+, walk, eat, drink, night-life)
- [2] Fara Poznsnska, church, must have (fine baroque, 30m+, free)
- [3] Plac Kolegiacki, (30min, short walk, free toilet)
- [6] Museum of applied arts (skip museum, straight to observation desk if weather is bad, view on old town)

- [8,11] Oldest part, lots of red brick, very long walk (no toilet warning, take taxi due to bridge replacement work)

- [12] Iperial district Castle, must have, worth quick look inside, Neo-Romanian sandstone (free entry, "not-a-museum" can walk inside freely, interesting interior, free map available inside is a must, free toilets, main hall was modernized, caffe, rest is "original") + classic neon sign next to main entrance 
- [13] Adam Mickiewicz Square (must have, hard to skip), 3min walk to opera house and old univestity building

- [18] Cytadela park (few hours walk, brick fort + soviet monuments, few ww2 and cold war tanks, cccp ww2 military cementary - worth looking how it is preserved - "we do not chase Russians with a baseball bat")

- [20] Old Brewery, Must have, award winning shopping mall, first large scale conversion of industial into art filled shopping center (few hours walk, heavy credit card usage danger)

- [26] Observation deck - best "birds eye view" on entire city + coffe


Not mentioned above, interesting architecture:
- New Theater [Dabrowskiego street](https://maps.app.goo.gl/y3Y4U6eNy4SK6rCb6) - funny name for 1906 building

- "Prusian wall" building [Jeżycka 6](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9CsmNK8gemQgJ3Vy9) - red brick with exposed wooden frame, designed to collapse when fired (Poznan was a fort city), there are only few here 

- Liberty square [Plac wolności](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Z36sEPzBQ9Uz11jX6)
    surrounded by mix of all styles of buildings, not populated by international turists, night life for locals hidden in nearby streets

All above (except 8,11,18) is "long walking" distance from 13 (if ordered properly), strict center, no remote locations. Only "easy to find" places. We have tiny marbles and "selfie backgrounds" scaterred - skipped here.
